WHY “AIRAVAT”

Born of the ocean.
At home in the sky.

In the old telling, Airavat rose from the churning of the cosmic ocean — a white elephant built to carry a king across any terrain, in any weather, without ever setting him down.

We took that name on purpose. Freight forwarding asks for the same range: the patience to move a container across an ocean, and the speed to move a shipment across the sky — without ever losing the load in between.

AGFL carries the same way. One name, every route, nothing too heavy for the plan.
